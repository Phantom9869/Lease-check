1. Term. This lease runs for twelve months and will automatically renew for successive twelve-month terms unless Tenant gives written notice ninety days before expiry.

2. Entry. Landlord may enter the unit at any time without notice to inspect or show the property.

3. Deposit. Tenant pays a security deposit equal to two months' rent. A cleaning fee of $250 is non-refundable.

4. Early termination. If Tenant ends the lease early, Tenant owes an early termination fee equal to three months' rent.

5. Repairs. Tenant is responsible for all repairs and maintenance, including structural and plumbing issues.

6. Liability. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury or loss, and Tenant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Landlord.

7. Late fees. A late fee of 15% of monthly rent applies after the 2nd of the month.

8. Rent changes. Landlord may adjust rent at any time upon notice at its sole discretion.

9. Occupants. Each tenant is jointly and severally liable for all rent.

10. Subletting. Tenant may not sublet or assign the unit.

11. Pets. No pets are allowed in the unit.
